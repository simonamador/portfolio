About

I'm Carlos Simón Amador Izaguirre, most people call me Simón. I'm a Peruvian biomedical engineer building reliable AI systems for healthcare and knowledge-intensive work, with a focus on production GenAI, medical computer vision, and biomedical research.

I studied at Tecnológico de Monterrey and spent six months at the Fetal Neonatal Neuroimaging and Developmental Science Center (FNNDSC) from Boston Children's Hospital and Harvard Medical School, where I co-authored research on deep generative models for fetal brain anomaly detection.

Currently, I build and scale internal GenAI systems at Laboratorios Bagó del Perú for pharmaceutical commercial and medical workflows. I work across RAG, evaluation, analytics, deployment, and cross-functional delivery.

My work connects production AI engineering with biomedical research. I welcome focused conversations about graduate study, research collaboration, and technical roles aligned with that intersection.
